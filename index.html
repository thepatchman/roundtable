Put a question on the table

The round

Every answer here comes from the model named beside it, through OpenRouter, billed to your own account. Nothing is impersonated and nothing is cached on a server — there is no server.

Models disagree less than you would hope. That is not a bug in the table, it is what these systems are like: trained to be agreeable, reading much the same internet. The useful part of a round is rarely the consensus. It is the one seat that breaks from it, and what it says next.
